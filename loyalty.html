GOOD TO KNOW

A Few Things You Might Be Wondering.

Find answers to common questions about joining, collecting leaves, and enjoying member rewards.

Joining takes less than a minute. Simply register online through our membership signup form or share your mobile number with our tea masters in person at the stall. You'll start earning loyalty points immediately with your very first cup.

Whenever you order at our stall or through our online catering services, mention your registered member number or show your digital membership pass. Eligible purchases automatically record points to your account.

You can review your active points balance, available tier perks, and claimable reward items right here on the Loyalty Page or by logging into your member profile. Our team can also look up your balance at the counter anytime.

Points can be redeemed across a generous selection of our regular chai varieties, handcrafted snacks, and seasonal gift merchandise. Please check the membership terms for the latest eligible rewards catalog.

We believe in unhurried enjoyment. Points remain active as long as you visit our stall or engage with our club within a continuous 12-month period. Please check the membership terms for the latest policy updates.

Yes. Your digital pass and member profile are universally recognized across all current and future Tea House locations, pop-up stalls, and catering kiosks.
